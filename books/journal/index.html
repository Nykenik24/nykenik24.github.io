My place.

Where I share rants, tips, small chapters of my life, new projects, amongst others.

This is not some kind of professional and ultra serious blog, or a way to share my incredible and not totally exaggerated development experiences for an impeccable portfolio: this is the place for me to emit my thoughts and personal experiences so people like you can read them.

If you are not interested in my life, that’s fine! You can skip this book entirely. At the end of the day the order in which I arranged my little shelf is not the important part, and not every book is meant to be read until the end. The part that matters is you, as the reader, having a good experience in my website. Not being impressed by it, but having fun.

My little shelf

You’ve probably read the introductory piece at the start of this book. If you didn’t, then that’s okay, I’ll briefly explain it.

The journal serves as a small place to share my personal experiences and my journey growing as a person, not strictly as a way to share my opinions, tips, to fill my portfolio or to farm professionality points like an over-obsessed shareholder or some bullshit inane nonsense like that.

Anyways, you might wonder what I mean by “my little shelf”; well, before this whole book thing there was a simpler website:

Much more minimalistic, much more techy, much more… everything. It was great, but not as great as this new bookshelf approach in my opinion.

This shelf represents my knowledge, my passion to learn and to spread the aforementioned knowledge with the world, with the people around me. This new structure represents me much more than the one this website had before and I just love it.
