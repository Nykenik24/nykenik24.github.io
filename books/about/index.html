Salutations, stranger.

I am Luca Andrei (a.k.a. Nykenik24), a hobby programmer mostly working with C/C++, Go, Lua, amongst others. I live somewhere in Spain, and I spend most of my free time building things I most likely did not need to.

I love to build open-source software to the degree you could say I crave for it. I think some of the best applications are completely community-driven FOSS and that freemium, SaaS, etc. are intrusive ways of anti-consumerism. No need to squeeze all money out of me to get your bread.

That is why all projects I make are free and open source, usually uploaded to Codeberg (well, using self-hosted Gitea recently, but that’s private).

Every day I try to become just a tad better at coding, a fact supported by my ability to quickly learn most things.

How to navigate this site.

This is pretty important, I recommend reading it!

At first this site might seem a bit confusing, at the end of the day it isn’t your day-to-day site.

Well, it’s pretty simple: each book can be clicked to see the cover, if you click the cover you rotate it and see a summary of the book and if you click the big OPEN button something you might’ve never expected happens: the book opens!

Once you are inside of a book you can pass pages with the arrows at the bottom (which you will need to do right now), close the book with the X button at the top-right corner or, if the book has a “contents” section at the start you can return to it by pressing the folder corner at the top-right corner (right below the close X).
