## _My_ place.

Where I share rants, tips, small chapters of my life, new projects, amongst others.

This is not some kind of professional and ultra serious blog, or a way to share
my incredible and not totally exaggerated development experiences for an impeccable
portfolio: this is _the_ place for me to emit my thoughts and personal experiences so
people like __you__ can read them.

If you are not interested in my life, that's fine! You can skip this book entirely.
At the end of the day the order in which I arranged my little shelf is not the
important part, and not every book is meant to be read until the end. The part that
matters is you, as the reader, having a good experience in my website. Not being
impressed by it, but having _fun_.
