# My little shelf

You've probably read the introductory piece at the start of this book. If you didn't,
then that's okay, I'll briefly explain it.

The journal serves as a small place to share __my personal experiences__ and __my 
journey growing as a person__, not strictly as a way to share my opinions, tips, to
fill my portfolio or to farm _professionality_ points like an over-obsessed shareholder
or some ~~bullshit~~ __inane nonsense__ like that.

Anyways, you might wonder what I mean by "my little shelf"; well, before this whole
book thing there was a simpler website:

{% figure "/assets/images/old_portfolio.png", "Old portfolio.", "Image of the old portfolio." %}

Much more minimalistic, much more techy, much more... _everything_. It was great, but not as great
as this new bookshelf approach in my opinion.

This shelf represents my knowledge, my passion to learn and to spread the aforementioned
knowledge with the world, with the people around me. This new structure represents me much
more than the one this website had before and I just __love it__.
