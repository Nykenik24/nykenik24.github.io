# How to navigate this site.

> This is pretty important, I recommend reading it!

At first this site might seem a bit confusing, at the end of the day it isn't
your day-to-day site.

Well, it's pretty simple: each book can be clicked to see the cover, if you click
the cover you rotate it and see a summary of the book and if you click the big
__OPEN__ button something you might've never expected happens: the book opens!

Once you are inside of a book you can pass pages with the arrows at the bottom 
(which you will need to do right now), close the book with the __X__ button at 
the top-right corner or, if the book has a "contents" section at the start you 
can return to it by pressing the folder corner at the top-right corner (right 
below the close __X__).
